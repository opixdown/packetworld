# Code of Conduct

PacketWorld is a learning project. People of every background and experience level should feel welcome to ask questions, make mistakes and contribute.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners. There are no stupid questions about networking.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully, focused on the work.
- Crediting others' ideas and contributions.
- Assuming good intent, and asking before concluding otherwise.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Gatekeeping or belittling someone's level of knowledge.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel run by the project) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository's GitHub profile, or by opening a private security advisory if no other channel is available. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they judge harmful.
